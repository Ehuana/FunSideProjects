Overview

What Is the EV Mode Mod?

In many markets outside the US, the Toyota Prius ships with a dedicated EV Mode button that lets the driver force the car into pure electric operation — perfect for quiet residential areas, parking garages, and short low-speed trips.

The US-spec Gen 2 Prius (2004–2009) has the exact same hardware lurking behind the dash — Toyota simply left out the button and wiring harness. The mod is straightforward:

Pull pin #27 on connector H14 to ground — that's it.

You can do it with a simple toggle switch, the headlight-flash trick, or the factory JDM EV button.

User Guide

From the Japanese Manual

Translated operating instructions for the factory EV mode.

In EV drive mode your vehicle runs like an electric vehicle, using only the electric motor powered by the hybrid battery. This mode is ideal for driving in residential areas early in the morning or late at night, and inside covered parking structures — no engine noise, no exhaust gases.

Press the switch to turn on electric motor power manually. The EV indicator light illuminates in the instrument cluster. Press again to turn it off.

The MFD will display a message and a beep will sound if any of these conditions exist:

  • ✕ Hybrid system overheated (hot weather, slope driving, high speed)
  • ✕ Hybrid system cold (left below 0 °C / 32 °F for a long time)
  • ✕ Engine still in warm-up cycle
  • ✕ Battery charge level ≤ 3 bars
  • ✕ Vehicle speed ≥ 45 km/h (28 mph)
  • ✕ Heavy throttle or steep incline
  • ✕ Windshield defroster is running

EV mode will automatically cancel (beep + indicator flashes 3×) if:

  • ⚠ Battery charge drops to ≤ 2 bars
  • ⚠ Speed exceeds ~45 km/h (28 mph)
  • ⚠ Aggressive throttle input or steep grade

On cold mornings, the ICE will begin its warm-up cycle roughly 10 seconds after the READY light appears. During warm-up, EV mode is blocked. Tip: press the EV button before the engine fires — there's a brief window after READY where EV mode can still be engaged.

The vehicle is nearly silent in EV mode. Pedestrians, cyclists, and other road users may not hear you approaching. Drive with extra caution and stay alert to your surroundings at all times.

The Prius is engineered for optimal fuel economy when the ICE and electric motor work together. Frequent use of EV mode can actually reduce overall efficiency — the battery still needs to be recharged by the engine. Use EV mode situationally, not as a default.
